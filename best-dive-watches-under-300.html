What Makes a Real Dive Watch?

At this price point, you should expect:

  • Minimum 200m water resistance: Anything less isn't a real dive watch
  • Screw-down crown: Essential for maintaining water resistance
  • Rotating bezel: For tracking dive time
  • Luminous markers: You need to read time underwater
  • Solid construction: These watches take abuse and keep going
Important: Under $300, you're looking at Japanese brands (Seiko, Orient, Citizen) and value-focused Swiss brands. You won't get Swiss luxury, but you will get watches that actually work as dive instruments.

Do You Actually Need 200m?

Real talk: unless you're actually scuba diving, 100m is plenty. But 200m-rated watches are built tougher overall. The screw-down crowns, the gaskets, the case construction – these watches are designed to take abuse. That's valuable even if you never go underwater.
